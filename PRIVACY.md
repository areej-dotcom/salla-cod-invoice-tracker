# Privacy Policy – Salla COD Invoice Tracker

**Last updated: November 5, 2026**

## Overview

Salla COD Invoice Tracker is a Chrome extension designed for authorized internal financial operations involving Cash on Delivery (COD) invoices on the Salla Naasa platform.

## Information Processed

The extension processes COD invoice information, including invoice identifiers, merchants, invoice dates, amounts, payment statuses, shipping companies, and invoice links.

## Data Storage

Invoice records, scanning progress, pending changes, and audit information are stored locally in the user's Chrome browser using Chrome extension storage.

The extension does not operate a separate cloud database or transmit invoice records to a developer-operated server.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used solely for internal invoice tracking, reconciliation, reporting, shipping company identification, and user-requested exports.

## Website Access

The extension accesses `https://nasa.salla.sa/` to retrieve relevant COD invoice information using the authorized user's existing session.

It does not modify invoice records in the Naasa platform.

## Exports and Backups

Users may manually export CSV reports and JSON backups. These files are saved to locations controlled by the user and should be handled according to their organization's data security policies.

## Data Sharing

The extension does not sell user data or automatically share invoice records with third-party services.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Invoice records remain in local browser storage until the user deletes them, restores a backup, or removes the extension and its associated local data.

The extension provides manual deletion controls with confirmation.

## Security

The extension is intended for authorized users. Access to Naasa requires an authenticated session, and users are responsible for protecting exported files and backups.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the extension's functionality or data practices.
